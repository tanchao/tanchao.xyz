MTS === Solopreneur under _current_ corporate organization.

With AI, an individual needs to shift their focus into: define the problem worth solving, gather evidence and align with stakeholders, spec, implementation, rollout and production continuously feedback+improvement.

The ownership and judgement call became obvious and transparent.

You can name it engineer or PM or DS, eventually the organization must support this shift; and the individual need to show and tell.
